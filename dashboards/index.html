8,000 orders, 12 cities, 7 cuisines, 3 channels. Churn risk means an order rated 2.5★ or lower. Filters recompute every chart from the raw orders.

Churn risk is a delivery-time problem. City, cuisine and discounts explain under 1% of it. Fulfilment time explains most of it: each extra minute raises churn odds by about 9%. Distance and rush hour matter only because they slow orders down.

Discounts cost 11% of GMV with no measurable rating effect. With no customer IDs, their effect on order frequency can't be tested, so a holdout test should come before renewal.

The delivery app costs restaurants about 17 points of margin. For the same basket, restaurants keep about 45% on dine-in, 35% on pickup and 28% on the app. Moving app orders to pickup recovers about 7 points, enough to fund a pickup incentive.
